# 💳 Fin Mitra — Merchant Financial Intelligence

**Fin Mitra** is a prototype web application designed to help small and micro merchants understand their business financial health, credit readiness, transaction safety, and loan affordability through simple, explainable tools.

> **Prototype / Demo:** Fin Mitra currently works with merchant-entered data and browser storage. It is not connected to banks, UPI networks, credit bureaus, or real lending systems.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📊 Merchant Dashboard

* Financial health overview
* Prototype credit score
* Monthly business volume
* Average daily transactions
* Payment success rate
* Active business days
* Business activity snapshot
* Personalized improvement suggestions
* Quick access to major features

### 👤 Merchant Profile

Merchants can enter:

* Name
* Shop / business name
* Mobile number
* Business type
* Business location
* PAN in demo format
* Monthly business income
* Average daily transactions
* Average transaction amount
* Failed transaction percentage
* Active business days
* Past loan behaviour

The application validates the entered information before saving the profile.

### 📈 Alternative Credit Score

Fin Mitra generates a **prototype credit score out of 900** using factors such as:

* Business activity regularity
* Transaction consistency
* Payment success
* Repayment behaviour
* Income signal

The score is designed to be transparent and explainable rather than a black-box result.

### 🛡️ UPI Safety / Fraud Protection

The safety checker identifies common transaction warning signals such as:

* Unusually large transactions
* New or unfamiliar recipients
* Urgency-based requests
* Unexpected collect requests
* Requests for OTP or UPI PIN

It provides different demo risk levels such as:

* Safer
* Needs Verification
* High Review Risk
* Potential Fraud Risk

### 💰 Loan & Repayment Planning

The loan planner provides prototype calculations for:

* Suggested loan range
* Estimated EMI
* Repayment capacity
* Recommended EMI ceiling
* Loan-to-income ratio
* Current overdue impact

The EMI calculation uses an example annual interest rate of **18%**.

### ⚙️ Settings

Users can control:

* Safety alerts
* Credit score reminders
* Saved prototype data

### 🌐 Multilingual Interface

The dashboard supports:

* English
* తెలుగు (Telugu)
* हिन्दी (Hindi)

## 🔐 Data & Privacy

Fin Mitra is currently a browser-based prototype.

Profile information and settings are stored using the browser's **localStorage**.

The application does **not** currently connect to:

* Banks
* UPI networks
* Credit bureaus
* Lending institutions
* External databases

Users should not enter real passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, or other confidential credentials.

---

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

Fin Mitra is a **prototype created for demonstration purposes**.

The credit score, fraud detection results, loan ranges, EMI calculations, and other classifications are demo outputs and should **not** be treated as:

* An official credit score
* A CIBIL score
* A real fraud-detection service
* A loan approval decision
* Financial advice
* A replacement for a bank, lender, or financial institution
